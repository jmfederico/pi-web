Show session notifications as one collapsible box at the end of the chat instead of a tray above it. The collapsed header carries the counts and the colour of the worst severity inside, so a notice is never hidden behind a silent height cap, and it stays readable while the rows scroll. Individual notices keep their full text and an always-visible dismiss control, and a Clear action removes them all at once.
