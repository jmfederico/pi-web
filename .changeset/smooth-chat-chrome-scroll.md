Float the chat header and composer over the transcript and slide them away on a downward scroll, so the transcript keeps its full height and reaches both borders instead of jumping or leaving an empty strip at either end. Session warnings and the notification inbox ride inside that header and leave with it, so their actions stay clickable and a stuck message header parks against the visible header edge rather than in the middle of a scrolled transcript. The transcript's scrollbar stays visible beside the composer.
